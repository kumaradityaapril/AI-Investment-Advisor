## Brand & Style

This design system expresses modern financial clarity through an airy, luminous aesthetic that transforms private wealth management into an intuitive, empowering ritual. Designed for forward-thinking consumers seeking high-tier portfolio oversight and intelligent forecasting, the interface avoids traditional Wall Street austerity and cold corporate density. 

The style blends pristine minimalism with subtle tactile dimensionality. Pure white surfaces hover over soft, ambient atmospheric backdrops. Crisp structural lines, pill-shaped tags, and luminous indigo-violet focal points project modern institutional capability balanced by approachable human warmth. Data is structured modularly rather than in dense, intimidating tabular blocks.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around radiant clarity and positive performance metrics:

- **Primary Canvas & Backgrounds:** The base canvas breathes with soft, cool luminosity, transitioning dynamically between `#f8fafc` (slate-50) and `#f1f5f9` (slate-100). Pure white (`#ffffff`) is reserved exclusively for elevated interactive cards, floating toolbars, and modal islands.
- **Primary & Secondary Violet/Indigo:** Deep indigo (`#4338ca`) serves as the core brand anchor for primary actions, active navigational states, and key financial headings. Modern electric violet (`#6366f1`) operates as an interactive hover counterpart, gradient accent, and AI synthesis highlight.
- **Tertiary Mint/Emerald:** Deep emerald (`#059669`) and vibrant mint (`#10b981`) represent positive yields, asset growth, AI optimizations, and completed targets. Muted mint surfaces (`#ecfdf5`) ground positive financial performance indicators.
- **Neutrals & Borders:** Neutral text utilizes slate-900 (`#0f172a`) for maximum readability, slate-600 (`#475569`) for analytical context, and slate-400 (`#94a3b8`) for secondary meta information. Border boundaries remain subtle at `#e2e8f0` (slate-200), providing definition without visual weight.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy harmonizes the warm, geometric optimism of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for titles, numerical displays, and badges with the utilitarian legibility of **Inter** for narrative guidance, transactional records, and data readouts.

- Numerical wealth figures prioritize tight negative letter tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) to deliver a modern editorial finish.
- Explanatory copy, AI financial insights, and contextual advisories prioritize generous line heights (`1.5` to `1.6`) to maintain an open, non-fatiguing reading experience.
- Labels, chips, and navigational tabs utilize uppercase or title-case Plus Jakarta Sans with subtle positive tracking to maintain crisp edge definition at small sizes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy centers on a responsive, modular card-grid anchored by floating horizontal structures. Heavy, rigid vertical sidebars are strictly omitted in favor of floating island headers and segmented navigation bars.

- **Grid Architecture:** Desktop displays utilize a 12-column responsive fluid grid maxing out at `1440px` with `2.5rem` outer canvas padding and `1.5rem` gutters. Tablet displays step down to an 8-column layout (`1rem` gutters), while mobile collapses into a single-column stacked hierarchy (`1rem` margins).
- **Rhythm & Whitespace:** Component padding relies on generous internal spacing (`space-lg` inside core cards) to ensure wealth charts, AI breakdowns, and allocations never feel claustrophobic.
- **Modular Breakpoints:** 
  - *Desktop (≥1024px):* Three-to-four modular card groupings side-by-side; floating horizontal top bar navigation.
  - *Tablet (768px – 1023px):* Two-column flow with persistent top segmented island navigation.
  - *Mobile (<768px):* Single card stack with a bottom-anchored, pill-shaped frosted navigation bar.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on soft ambient luminance, gentle border delineation, and floating planes rather than aggressive, high-opacity drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Underlying smooth gradient canvas (`#f8fafc` to `#f1f5f9`).
- **Level 1 (Modular Surface Cards):** Pure white background (`#ffffff`), reinforced by a razor-thin structural stroke (`1px solid #f1f5f9` or `#e2e8f0`) and an ambient tinted drop shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Interactive Modules):** Gentle elevation gain with slight vertical lift (`translateY(-2px)`), paired with a widened shadow: `0 12px 28px -4px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.08), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Floating Island Navigation & Drawers):** High-blur frosted glass backing (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` with `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`), held together by a crisp border (`1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`) and ambient shadow: `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.07)`.

## Shapes

The shape system leverages harmonious, rounded geometric forms that convey approachability without losing institutional precision.

- **Cards & Modules:** Configured at `rounded-lg` (16px / `1rem`), providing smooth corners that naturally frame complex financial data, graphs, and metric modules.
- **Large Panels & Hero Banners:** Scale to `rounded-xl` (24px / `1.5rem`) for expansive portfolio overviews and conversational AI interaction modules.
- **Interactive Badges, Metric Chips & Pills:** Apply full pill curvature (`9999px`) to create tactile, soft touchpoints for portfolio status, trend percentages, and filter controls.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled in deep indigo (`#4338ca`), transitioning on hover to vibrant violet (`#6366f1`). Styled with `rounded-md` (8px) or pill shapes, featuring bold Plus Jakarta Sans text and subtle indigo box glows on interaction.
- **Secondary / Soft:** Solid pale indigo background (`#e0e7ff` / slate-100) with primary indigo text (`#4338ca`). No heavy borders.
- **Ghost:** Transparent background with subtle slate-600 hover states; used exclusively for auxiliary utility toggles.

### Metric Chips & Pills
- **Trend Positive:** Full-pill containers with pale mint backgrounds (`#ecfdf5`), saturated emerald text (`#059669`), and leading upward trajectory micro-icons.
- **AI Recommendation Chips:** Subtle gradient borders (`#6366f1` to `#10b981`) paired with frosted white centers, highlighting automated rebalancing suggestions and automated tax harvesting insights.

### Cards & Modular Containers
- Pure white surfaces featuring `1rem` corner rounding, internal padding of `1.5rem`, and delicate outline borders (`#e2e8f0`). 
- Internal headers host intuitive plain-language summaries (e.g., "Net Worth Momentum", "Retirement Projection") flanked by micro status indicators instead of dense tabular labels.

### Floating Island Navigation
- Suspended top navigation capsule floating `1rem` below the viewport top. Houses brand identity, profile balance summary, segmented tab navigation ("Overview", "Holdings", "Forecast", "Ask AI"), and contextual alert triggers within a single unified pill container.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Minimalist rounded troughs styled with `#f8fafc` surface fills, subtle borders (`#cbd5e1`), and generous internal horizontal padding (`1rem`). On focus, shifts to a pure white fill with a 2px soft ring in secondary indigo (`#6366f1`).

### Lists & Transaction Feeds
- Structured as separated floating row-items or soft pill-divided cards rather than contiguous alternating-row spreadsheets. Every line-item features circular vendor/asset iconography, prominent plain-text descriptions, and crisp currency values.
