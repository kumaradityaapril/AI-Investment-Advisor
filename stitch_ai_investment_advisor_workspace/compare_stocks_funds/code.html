Financial Terms Explained Simply

No Wall Street jargon. Just what it means for your real money.

Beginner Friendly 💡
What is P/E Ratio? sell

Think of it as the price tag on an investment's profits. A higher number means investors are paying more upfront because they expect rapid growth tomorrow.

Simple rule of thumb: Under 20x = Bargain/Slow | Over 35x = High Growth Premium
What is Debt-to-Equity? shield

How much borrowed money the company relies on to stay in business. Lower debt means management can sleep peacefully even during high interest rates and recessions.

Simple rule of thumb: Under 1.0x = Safe & Sound | Over 2.5x = Heavily Leveraged
What is Net Margin? savings

Out of every $100 earned from customers, how many dollars stay as clean, unspent profit after paying all staff, factories, servers, and taxes.

Simple rule of thumb: Over 20% = Elite Cash Machine | Under 5% = Thin Cushion
